Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit this portfolio website and how it is protected.

01

Introduction

Welcome to the personal portfolio website of Aman Singh. This Privacy Policy describes how personal information you voluntarily share through the contact form or direct communication is handled.

By browsing this website or submitting a message, you agree to the simple and transparent data practices outlined below.

02

Information We Collect

Only minimal information necessary to respond to your inquiries and maintain website functionality is collected:

Personal Information

When you fill out the contact form on the portfolio page, the following details may be collected:

  • Your Name
  • Your Email Address
  • Message Subject
  • Project, internship, or inquiry details you include in your message

Automated Technical Data

Standard web server logs may temporarily record basic technical details for security and performance:

  • Browser type and device viewport size
  • Pages visited and referring links
  • Date and time of access
  • General server error diagnostics
03

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website is used strictly for professional communication:

Responding to your messages and questions
Discussing job or internship opportunities
Collaborating on web or software projects
Improving website layout and user experience
Preventing spam or unauthorized form abuse
Maintaining reliable site performance
04

Information Sharing and Disclosure

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or traded to third parties for marketing purposes.

With Your Consent

Details you share are kept private and are only used to communicate directly with you regarding your request or project collaboration.

External Links & Legal Compliance

Please keep the following in mind when using external links on this site:

  • Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, or Facebook lead to third-party platforms with their own privacy policies
  • Information may only be disclosed if strictly required by applicable law
05

Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are taken to safeguard any messages or contact details you submit:

Secure Transmission

Form inputs are validated and transmitted safely to prevent unauthorized interception.

Restricted Access

Only the portfolio owner has access to inquiries sent via the contact form.

Minimal Retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed for ongoing communication.

06

Your Rights and Choices

You remain in control of any personal details you share through this website:

Access Your Data

Request confirmation of any contact details you previously submitted.

Correct Information

Ask to update or correct your email address or inquiry details.

Delete Your Data

Request removal of your past messages from email records at any time.

Opt Out

Choose not to submit personal details through the form and connect via social profiles instead.

07

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally as new projects, features, or portfolio sections are added.

Any updates will be reflected on this page along with the revised date at the top.
